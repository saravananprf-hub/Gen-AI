What is RAG in AI?

    -->RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
    -->RAG is an AI technique that allows a Large Language Model (LLM) to retrieve information from 
external documents or databases and use that information to generate accurate, relevant answers.
    -->In simple words, RAG helps an AI answer questions using your own data instead of relying only on the information it learned during training.

What is Embedding in NLP?
    -->Embedding is the process of converting text, words, or other data into numerical vectors
    that machines can understand.
    -->In simple words, computers understand numbers better than human language.
    Embeddings convert words or sentences into numbers while capturing their meaning and relationships.
